Update your Plane Radar from a computer

Use this page if your radar keeps restarting and cannot finish an update on its own. It installs the current firmware directly over the USB cable, so it works even when the radar is restarting every couple of minutes.

Your WiFi details and settings are kept. This replaces only the program, not your setup, so there is nothing to enter again afterwards.

You will need Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, on a computer. This cannot work in Safari or Firefox, or on a phone or tablet.

Steps

  1. Plug the radar into your computer or laptop with the USB-C cable. Leave the radar switched on — it is fine if it is restarting.
  2. The first time you plug it in, your computer may ask about a new accessory — click Allow to let it connect.
  3. Press Update my radar below.
  4. A small window appears listing devices. Choose the one that appears when you plug the radar in, then press Connect. On Windows it is often called USB Serial Device; on a Mac, usbmodem followed by some numbers.
  5. Wait about two minutes without unplugging anything. The radar restarts on its own when it has finished, and the update is done.
