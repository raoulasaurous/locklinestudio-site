Update your Plane Radar from a computer

Use this page if your radar keeps restarting and cannot finish an update on its own. It installs the current firmware directly over the USB cable, so it works even when the radar is restarting every couple of minutes.

Your WiFi details and settings are kept. This replaces only the program, not your setup, so there is nothing to enter again afterwards.

What you need

  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, on a computer. This cannot work in Safari or Firefox, or on a phone or tablet — those browsers are not allowed to talk to USB devices.
  • A USB-C cable that carries data. Many charging cables only carry power. If your radar does not appear in the list later, this is almost always why — try the cable that came with a phone or an external drive.

Steps

  1. Plug the radar into your computer with the USB-C cable. Leave it switched on — it is fine if it is restarting.
  2. Press Update my radar below.
  3. A small window appears listing devices. Choose the one that appears when you plug the radar in, then press Connect. On Windows it is often called USB Serial Device; on a Mac, usbmodem followed by some numbers.
  4. Wait about two minutes without unplugging anything. The radar restarts on its own when it has finished.

This browser cannot do it. Please open this same page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, on a computer rather than a phone. This page needs a secure connection. Please open it as https://locklinestudio.com/radar/update/.

If it does not work

The radar is not in the list. Nine times out of ten the cable is charge-only. Try a different one before anything else. Failing that, try the other USB port, and unplug any hub.

It stops partway through. Nothing is broken — the radar keeps the program it already had. Unplug it, plug it back in and start again.

Still stuck. Email support@locklinestudio.com and say what the screen shows. A real person answers.

What this actually does

It writes the current released firmware into the radar and points it at that copy on the next start. It does not erase the area holding your WiFi credentials, your location or your preferences.

The firmware it installs is the same public build the radar downloads for itself, published at our releases page. Nothing is uploaded from your computer to us, and the transfer happens entirely between your browser and the radar in front of you.
