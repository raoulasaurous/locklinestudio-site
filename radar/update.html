Update your Plane Radar from a computer

Use this page if your radar keeps restarting and cannot finish an update on its own. It installs the current firmware directly over the USB cable, so it works even when the radar is restarting every couple of minutes.

The radar restarts like a brand-new unit afterwards. It will ask to be connected to your WiFi again, exactly like the day it arrived — that takes a minute and is expected.

You will need Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, on a computer. This cannot work in Safari or Firefox, or on a phone or tablet.

Steps

  1. Plug the radar into your computer with its USB-C cable. Leave the radar switched on - it is fine if it is restarting.
  2. If your computer asks about a new accessory, click Allow. This usually only happens the first time.
  3. Type the code from our message into the box below and press Unlock.
  4. Press the blue Update my radar button.
  5. A popup lists the ports on your computer - pick the radar and press Connect. This popup is normal: it is the browser asking your permission.
    • On a Mac the radar is called USB JTAG/serial debug unit.
    • On Windows it is usually USB Serial Device.
    • Not sure? Press Cancel, unplug the radar, and open the popup again - the entry that disappeared is the radar.
  6. Press Install when it is offered, and leave everything plugged in for about two minutes. When it finishes, unplug the radar and plug it back in.
  7. The radar starts up asking for WiFi, like the day it arrived. Connect it to your WiFi and you are done - it is now on the latest version.

If you accidentally close the popup, a screen appears saying "No port selected" and mentioning drivers. Ignore the drivers - your radar does not need any. Just press Try Again and pick the radar from the list.

Unlock

Enter the code from your support email, then the update button appears.
